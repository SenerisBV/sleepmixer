In the towns where Aurish is spoken, they ring the bells all night, softly, one every so often, so the boats know where the shore is. Nobody minds. People say they can't sleep without them. A child who can't sleep is told: hanu ta iskel, o? Do you hear the bells? And the child says, va. And then the child is asleep.
